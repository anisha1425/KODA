The Silent Echo

by Eleanor Rigby
star star star star star_half
4.8 (2.4k reviews)
menu_book 384 Pages
language English
calendar_today Oct 2023

In the misty harbor town of Oakhaven, silence isn't just an absence of sound—it's a presence. When Clara Vance, a disgraced investigative journalist, returns to her childhood home to bury her estranged father, she discovers a manuscript hidden in his study. The pages tell a story that mirrors a cold case from thirty years ago, a disappearance that haunts the town to this day.

As Clara digs deeper, the line between fiction and reality blurs. Whispers in the fog, unexplainable echoes in empty rooms, and a town that seems desperate to forget its past. "The Silent Echo" is a haunting exploration of memory, guilt, and the noise we make to drown out the truth.

About the Author

Eleanor Rigby

Eleanor Rigby is an award-winning author of contemporary mystery and gothic thrillers. Her debut novel, "The Velvet Night," was a New York Times bestseller. She lives in a renovated lighthouse on the coast of Maine with her two cats and an excessive collection of vintage typewriters.
