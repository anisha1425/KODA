The Midnight Echo

By Eleanor Vane |
star star star star star_half
(128 Reviews)

"In the quiet town of Raven's Hollow, silence is the loudest scream."

When investigative journalist Sarah Jenkins returns to her childhood home to settle her late aunt's estate, she expects nothing more than dust and old memories. But a discovered diary reveals a century-old secret that connects the town's founding families to a series of unexplained disappearances.

As the midnight hour approaches each night, a strange echo reverberates through the valley—a sound that shouldn't exist. Sarah must decipher the cryptic warnings in the diary before the echo claims its next victim: her.

The Midnight Echo weaves a tale of atmospheric horror and gripping suspense, challenging the boundary between reality and the supernatural.
